00 — How To Use This Program

Open Today's Day. Do It. Close The Page.

The strip up top is the rhythm these thirty days build. Each day below adds one piece of it. Five rules for using this page:

  1. 1
    Only today. Each morning, open the first Day with an empty circle left on it and do its tasks. Don't read ahead and don't plan the week. The Day in front of you is the whole job.
  2. 2
    A Day isn't a calendar day. Each Day is sized to fit in one, but it's finished when its tasks are finished, not at midnight. If a Day takes you two, fine. The next Day starts only when this one is closed.
  3. 3
    Check it off. Every circle is a task. The Day is done when every circle on it is done.
  4. 4
    Yesterday keeps running. Each new Day adds to the ones before it. Nothing you've installed gets dropped.
  5. 5
    Check in, unprompted. Your check-ins are what I coach from, so send them without being asked. We do this over Telegram: when you close a Day, tell me how it went. When a Day asks for a photo, a pick, or an answer, send it there the same day. If something isn't working, say so immediately. That's what I'm here for.

Day 0 — Get Set Up

Three Things Today. Then You Start.

No food changes yet. Today is just tools and picks.

  1. 1
    The scale. Buy a digital kitchen food scale that measures in grams. Any brand under $20 works. Get it today (in-store or same-day). It's the one tool everything else needs.
  2. 2
    Yogurt recon. Buy plain 0% Greek yogurt, a few different brands. The only filter: the ingredients read milk + live cultures, nothing else. No added sugar, no "vanilla," no "honey." Those flavors hide 10–15g of sugar. Send a photo of each tub's label.
  3. 3
    Pick your proteins. From the list below, tell me your top 3: just the three you'd actually eat. It's not exhaustive; if you eat something lean that's missing, say so.
Pick Your Proteins
Tuna / chicken pouch (in water) Pre-cooked chicken pouch Frozen grilled chicken strips Rotisserie chicken Hard-boiled eggs Deli turkey breast Frozen white fish Frozen salmon Lean beef (90/10)

Day 1 — Water In, Yogurt In

Two Easy Installs.

  • ›
    Every morning from now on. On waking, drink 2 liters of water, before coffee and before food. Big bottle, first thing. That's the whole task. (No bottle with a steel or glass interior? Grab a good one today.)
  • ›
    Start the yogurt. Start eating the yogurt. Any amount counts: a spoonful or half a tub. You bought a few brands: find the one you genuinely like. That's the one we build on.

Day 2 — The Evening Ritual + Your First Meat

Tonight, You Prep Tomorrow's First Food.

This is where the structure starts doing the work for you.

  • ›
    The evening ritual · tonight, then every night. Weigh one serving of yogurt (serving size is on the tub) into a container, lid on, into the fridge. That's tomorrow's first food, already waiting. Your day starts itself: wake → water → the yogurt that's sitting there, whenever you start eating.
  • ›
    The meat run · buy, cook, eat today. From your top 3, pick one. Buy enough for the week, prepare it, and eat it. Today. You're in the driver's seat on how it's made: seasoning, sides, your taste. Already-cooked options make this easy. Then send a quick report: a photo, what you picked, how you prepped it, and where you bought it.

Need Ideas?

Recipes, seasonings, cooking methods, easy shortcuts. If you're stuck on any of it, just ask.

Day 3 — Taste Check

Does It Actually Taste Good?

A lean meat you only tolerate disappears from your plate by week two, so we fix taste now, before it becomes a problem.

Be honest: "it was fine" counts as a no. Three ways this can go:

  • ›
    You liked it. Great, keep it. We can still tune the seasoning if you want.
  • ›
    Close but not there. Let's make it better: seasoning, a sauce, a different cooking method. Small fixes beat starting over.
  • ›
    Not working. Swap it for another pick. (Easiest option on the board: pre-cooked chicken breast strips. No prep, hard to get wrong.)

If you swap, just repeat the meat run today with the new pick: buy, cook, eat, report.

Day 4 — Portion The Meat

The Ritual Grows One Step.

One thing worth knowing: meat is mostly water, so grams on the scale isn't grams of protein. You don't need the math, just a portion size.

  • ›
    Tonight, then every night. After the yogurt, weigh out one portion of your cooked meat and add it to the fridge, ready to grab tomorrow. Scale tip: put the empty container on, hit tare/zero, then add the food so the scale reads just the meat.

⚠ The One Strict Rule

Your prepped food is always the first food you eat each day. Water first, then the containers, before anything else. Be strict about this one. Everything you eat after it is still completely yours.

Day 5 — A Vegetable Joins The Stack

The One Food That Makes Everything After It Easier.

  1. 1

  2. 2
    Then, buy it, learn it, stack it. Grab frozen steam-in-bag vegetables, taste it today, and tonight add a serving to the prep, alongside the yogurt and the meat. Three containers now.

A Breather, And A Look Ahead.

Five days of stacking earns a day to close loose ends. Nothing new lands today.

Anything from Day 0–5 that's still open, we finish. If something isn't clicking (a meat that's stalling, a step that keeps slipping), we talk it through and fix it.

What's Next

Weeks 2–4 are personalized to you. I'll add one small change at a time, based on how your check-ins go: a bit more protein, a portion tweak, a longer morning window, some daily walking. Nothing to memorize now; you'll get each step when it's your turn. And tell me how you're feeling. That's the most useful thing you can report.
